Cronome: Introduction

7/7/2025

Welcome to my latest project! I would like to introduce you to a world that I've been cooking up:
Cronome is world, an old world. Over 100 billion years old to be infact. This is a world which asks one question: What if life was allowed to continue existing for billions of years without interuption? Here on Earth, life has only existed for less than 4 billion years, and its really only been noticable for the last 540 million years. More than that, its been estimated that Earth will only be able to sustain life for the next billion years or so. Taking this all into account, the best estimates we have indicate that life will be a phenomenon on Earth for give or take 5 billion years, and noticable life will only have been a thing for around 1.5 billion years. This is where Cronome comes in, Cronome is a unique world in that it meets all the conditions for life but won't be in danger of losing it for a long, long time.
Like on Earth, a highly intelligent genus of creatures known as the 'Dominants' has emerged as the dominant species, they have learned to bend the world to their will and have established complex societies with equally complex relationships. However unlike on Earth, the large timeframe on their dominance means that they are constantly evolving and even forming different, unique species within their genus. The world they have created has also shaped how life around it has evolved, new organisms have emerged to fill in the new niches created by the dominants. Imagine organisms living to feed on the electricity that runs through our telephone poles, new parasites which can survive for days in asphalt parking lots, entire ecosystems that prop up in the unique environment created by warzones.. But don't think of it as just 'Earth but with new animals', no, I'm working hard to make a highly realistic, dynamic, and intricate world that spans across a massive timeframe. I'm working to design and determine things like the history of the solar system, the composition of the atmosphere, the evolution of the tectonic plates, the trees (yes, trees plural) of life, the history of the Dominants, their culture, their society, their religion, their challenges, extraterrestrial occurances, and more..
This project will take a long time to cook and really there are three ways I see it going:
1. I realize the scope is too large, I get bored, I abandon it.
2. It consumes my life, I work hard on it nonstop constantly.
3. This is the one I'm hoping for, I hope to have it a passion side project that I always come back to when I want to apply something that I've recently learned. I don't want it be all I do, but I want to stick to it also, and always have it there for me.
With all that said, I thank you for reading and welcome to Cronome!
