Starting A Massive Project On A Whim? What's the Worst That Could Happen

Some Thoughts

Everyone at some point has experienced that “3AM motivation.” Imagine: It’s some point late in the night, you're alone, and it’s just you and your thoughts. At such times your mind begins to wander. And it’s in the wandering that we often find those sudden bursts of creativity and inspiration.

Unfortunately for the midnight idealist however it’s often the case that reality tempers these dreams. One wakes up the next day and wonders how such a dream could be possible for them. The plans drawn by the drowsy mind seem worn out by the time the sunlight shines on them.

But this doesn’t have to be the case.

Oftentimes our commitments to these plans are half-hearted at best. We get our spike of dopamine from dreaming, then we lay down our heads, satisfied, and right there is the moment that our plans meet their demise. We have to remove the satisfaction from the planning and instead get that satisfaction from the action. And of course this is easier said than done.

An Announcement

And to prove that it can be done I’ve decided to take this action myself. One night that motivation struck me, what if I became a game developer? I pondered this idea as my rational side responded to the obvious fact that I had no knowledge of what this would take, still though the idea wouldn’t leave my head. What was the solution? The solution was not to ignore that rational side nor to give up on the idea but to make a compromise. Before I could gain any satisfaction from my act of planning I would commit to taking the first step.

And so I installed a game engine, and I found tutorials, and got to work on this idea I was hatching. And lastly to finally my commitment I wrote this silly article to announce my project and also make it work as an advice column to myself.

Which I guess is a more insightful way to announce that you're working on a new project. I’ll post the details of it once I’m sure I have it figured out. I’ve always wanted to try developing a game, lots involved with it. But by taking a first step and announcing my intentions I could overcome the struggle of losing my ideas to a night of sleep.
