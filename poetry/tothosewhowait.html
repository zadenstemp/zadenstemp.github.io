To Those Who Wait

By Zaden 5/1/2025

I speak directed now at those who wait,
The days before are long and springs outside,
The sun is hot and sounds of birds who hide,
In trees of May that soon in June they'll hate.

For life today is warm and tragic fate,
Awaits for those who take but don't abide,
The posted sign of Father Time who's ride,
Continues through the heat and nevers late.

To June and more the wish now true in heat,
Oppressive stands you peering back to May,
A stage of flowers still in winds of sleet,
Inside the mind of some far June today,
Alas your blidness seen is summer's feat,
A peaceful rain but seeing naught but grey.
